# Editorial commissioning quality

Automated commissioning must not turn a new worksheet, reading duration or generic
competitor label into a new canonical intent. A vendor library or historical book
is not evidence of a competing consumer product.

1. Give the commissioning editor actual article descriptions, section coverage,
   existing comparison owners and queued reader outcomes.
2. Require a complete per-candidate ownership decision. Missing, duplicated or
   unknown decisions fail closed. Prefer merging an overlapping idea into its
   existing owner; do not enqueue it under another slug.
3. Bind new comparisons to an explicitly researched, named vendor and its unique
   canonical owner. Preserve rejected proposals and review evidence privately.
4. Exercise these gates with unit and native Workflow/D1/R2 checks, then obtain a
   read-only Grok review. Deploy only verified code and read production state back.

Backlog targets never override quality gates. A shortfall remains visible when
there are too few supported, distinct reader tasks. Existing failed attempts and
quarantined candidates remain in the audit history.
