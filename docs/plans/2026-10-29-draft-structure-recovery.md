# Bounded recovery for draft JSON framing

The producer can consume its three writing rounds on otherwise complete responses whose only serialization defect is an extra root closer or a missing `sections` array closer immediately before an edition's `figure` key. Such responses previously required another model generation before any independent review could run.

Recover only these observed structures in draft output marked `invalid_json_syntax`. A strict tokenizer/parser can insert at most two `]` tokens, at the `zh.sections` / `en.sections` boundary before `figure`, and remove at most four closing delimiters after a complete root object. It rejects incomplete strings/root objects, invalid escapes, comments, other trailing content, unrelated missing delimiters and excessive nesting/length. The sequence of every quoted string, key and primitive must remain byte-for-byte identical. No words, values or field names are generated or dropped.

Keep the original malformed response, the structural edit record and the recovered value in the existing private per-round archive. The full normal schema, content/source checks and separate factual/usefulness/originality/bilingual review still run. Recovering grammar cannot turn a rejected claim into an accepted claim. Reviewer output and planning output do not use this recovery. Unsupported defects keep the existing bounded model-repair path; all round, attempt and request budgets remain unchanged.

Verification covers the two observed forms, combined Chinese/English cases, quoted delimiters and refusal cases. Native Cloudflare Workflows/D1/R2 checks exercise recovery without another model call, retain the original raw record, and still block an article rejected by the separate reviewer after all three rounds. Private production artifacts may be replayed offline to verify framing recovery, but this never republishes or restarts an old failed run. A new deployment does not prove a future natural run published; read its actual terminal result separately.
