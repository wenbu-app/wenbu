# Bounded editorial review protocol repair

An invalid review schema currently replaces useful criticism with schema diagnostics. The next round then asks the article writer to repair the review's representation. Rewriting an article cannot fix an overlong review locator. A malformed final review still correctly blocks publication; fixing the representation alone does not resolve its substantive objections.

Keep three rounds and the existing physical request/day limits. When the draft is valid but the reviewer response is not, use the next existing round to review the unchanged draft again. Supply field diagnostics and bounded previous criticism as untrusted feedback. Preserve complete raw output and validation evidence in private R2. Do not normalize a malformed response into approval. Once a valid review identifies an article defect, use the ordinary writer repair followed by independent review. Repeated invalid reviews exhaust the bound and block publication.

Verification covers unchanged draft identity, writer/reviewer call counts and reservations, preservation of negative feedback, continued factual rejection, publication only after valid approval, and retained raw invalid attempts. No production model probe, quality threshold change, budget reset or extra scheduler is part of this repair.
